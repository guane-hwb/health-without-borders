## International Classification of Diseases (ICD) — World Health Organization

| File | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `app/data/icd10_codes.json` | ICD-10, WHO 2019 edition: code → display (English from WHO; Spanish display names where the Colombian Vulcano IHCE fragment provides them) | WHO ICD API, `https://id.who.int/icd/release/10/2019`; Ministry of Health of Colombia, Vulcano IHCE implementation guide (`vulcano.ihcecol.gov.co`) |
| `app/data/icd11_codes.json` | ICD-11 for Mortality and Morbidity Statistics (MMS), release 2024-01, Spanish: code → display | WHO ICD API, `https://id.who.int/icd/release/11/2024-01/mms` |
