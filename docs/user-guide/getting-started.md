# Getting Started

This page covers logging in, the home screen, changing the language, and signing out safely.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the app. On the login screen, enter the **email and password** provided by your organization administrator.
2. Tap **Log in**.

Your access depends on your **role** (for example, medical staff, or an organization administrator). Administrators see additional options such as **Statistics**, **Manage users**, and **Organizations**.

**Note:** Once you are logged in, the app keeps your session active as you move between screens. If your session expires, you will be asked to sign in again — any records you registered in the field are **kept on the device** until they can be synchronized.

---

## 2. The home screen

From the home screen you can reach the main actions:

- **New patient** — register a patient and assign a bracelet.
- **Search patients** — find an existing patient.
- **Pending to sync** — review records waiting to be uploaded. A **gold badge** shows the number of records still stored locally (including any duplicates or errors).

Administrators also see **Statistics**, **Manage users**, and **Organizations**.

---

## 3. Changing the language

The app is bilingual. Use the **ES / EN** switch in the header to change the language instantly — titles, buttons, filters, and cards update immediately, with no need to restart the screen. Dates are also shown in the format that matches the selected language.

The language switch is available on the main screens, including New patient, Search patients, Pending to sync, Manage users, and Organizations.

---

## 4. Signing out

1. Open the menu and tap **Log out**.
2. If you have records **pending synchronization**, the confirmation dialog will warn you and recommend syncing before you leave. When possible, [sync your pending records](sync-pending.md) first so nothing is left only on the device.
